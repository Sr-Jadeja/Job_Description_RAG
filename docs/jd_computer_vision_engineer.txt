Job Title: Computer Vision Engineer
Company: VisionTech Labs
Location: Chennai, India (On-site)

- Deploy and monitor computer vision models in production

Required Skills:

- Experience with OpenCV and image processing

- Knowledge of CNNs, object detection, and image segmentation
- Understanding of model evaluation and optimization
- Familiarity with Git and Docker

Good to Have:
- Experience with YOLO or Detectron2
- Knowledge of OCR systems
- Experience with edge AI deployment
- Familiarity with cloud platforms
- Experience with video analytics
