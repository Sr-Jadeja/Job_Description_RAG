Job Title: NLP Engineer
Company: LanguageAI Labs
Location: Pune, India (On-site)
Experience: 2-5 years

About the Role:
We are building next-generation conversational AI products and need an NLP Engineer to join our core team. You will work on everything from text classification to LLM fine-tuning.

Responsibilities:
- Design and build NLP pipelines for text classification, NER, sentiment analysis, and summarization
- Fine-tune pre-trained transformer models (BERT, RoBERTa, LLaMA) for domain-specific tasks
- Build and maintain RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipelines using LangChain or LlamaIndex
- Evaluate model performance using standard NLP benchmarks
- Collaborate with product teams to integrate NLP features into applications
- Stay updated with latest research papers and implement relevant techniques

Required Skills:
- Strong Python and NLP fundamentals
- Experience with HuggingFace Transformers library
- Knowledge of embedding models and vector databases (FAISS, Pinecone, Weaviate)
- Experience building RAG pipelines with LangChain or LlamaIndex
- Understanding of LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq)
- Experience with text preprocessing (tokenization, lemmatization, stopword removal)

Good to Have:
- Experience fine-tuning LLMs using LoRA or QLoRA
- Knowledge of prompt engineering techniques
- Familiarity with evaluation frameworks (RAGAS, TruLens)
- Published research or open-source contributions in NLP

Compensation: INR 15-25 LPA
